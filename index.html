About Us

A local team you can trust with your biggest investment

Surya Architect and Builders has served Gajwel and the Siddipet district since 2018. We are architects and builders in one firm, so your design, approvals, structure and construction are all handled by the same people who know your project.

Building a home is a big step for any family. We keep it simple, explain every stage clearly, and build with the care we would give our own homes.
