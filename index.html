Every Home Has a Story

And every story begins with a dream: a home for your family, a shop for your business, a building that will last for generations. Before you start, you probably have questions like:

  • "How much will my house cost to build?"
  • "Is my plan correct as per vastu?"
  • "How do I get building permission?"
  • "Is my structure strong and safe?"

At Surya Architect and Builders, we answer these questions every day. Since 2018, families and businesses in Gajwel and Siddipet district have trusted us to plan, design, get approvals for and build their homes, all under one roof.
